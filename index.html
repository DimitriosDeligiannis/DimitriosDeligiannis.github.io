BAJA SAE — 2027 “Leo”

Suspension & Steering

Designed and fabricated custom tie rods and rear toe links for our 2027 vehicle, Leo, using 4130 chromoly steel tubing with threaded weld bungs butt-welded to accommodate high-misalignment heim joints. This configuration significantly expanded angular articulation through full suspension travel, eliminating mechanical bind during severe bump and droop. By optimizing link lengths and mounting hardpoints, the geometry targets an aggressive static camber profile of -1° to -3°, ensuring dynamic camber gain compensates for chassis roll so the tire contact patch remains flat under heavy cornering loads.

Key Focus: Kinematic modeling, tensile/buckling load analysis, and packaging integration within the uprights and chassis hardpoints.
